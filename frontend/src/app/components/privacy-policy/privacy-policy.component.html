Privacy Policy

Public blockchain lookups

Ethereum addresses, transaction hashes, and block identifiers submitted to the explorer are public blockchain data. Requests are processed by eth.tx.taxi and its configured public Ethereum data providers.

Local preferences

Display preferences such as language, timezone, fiat currency, amount mode, and theme may be stored in your browser. These preferences are used to render the interface and are not Ethereum account credentials.

Operational records

The hosting stack and upstream data providers may retain routine security and request logs. Do not use a public explorer when a lookup itself is sensitive; use your own Ethereum node and local tooling instead.

External links

Links to Ethereum documentation, Telegram, u.software, Blockscout, and the upstream Mempool project are governed by those services' own privacy policies.
